# 007 — MIT license and no required services

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-21

## Context
Developers must never regret building on the engine.

## Decision
MIT, with no royalties, accounts, telemetry or required cloud.

## Alternatives
- Source-available or revenue share: why not, it recreates the risk developers fear.
- Copyleft: why not, it would constrain how games are licensed.

## Consequences
Runtime dependencies must be permissive. See [no regrets](../idea/31-no-regrets.md).
